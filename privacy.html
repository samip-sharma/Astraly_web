Privacy Policy

Astraly · Effective September 24, 2026 · Contact: privacy@astraly.app

Astraly (“we”, “us”) is a personal astrology companion app. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies to the Astraly mobile apps and related API services.

Information we collect

  • Birth details you provide — name or nickname, birth date, optional birth time, and birth city (including coordinates and time zone when you select a city).
  • Conversation content — questions you ask and generated replies, so your chat history can be shown again on this device or after account recovery.
  • Account identifiers — if you choose to connect Google or Apple, we store a stable provider subject identifier (not your password). Email may be available from the provider but is not required for Astraly to work.
  • Subscription status — membership state verified through our billing provider (RevenueCat) and the app stores. We do not receive your full payment card number.
  • Device signals needed to operate the product — guest session credential, push notification token (if you allow notifications), device time zone for daily notes, and basic app/platform metadata.
  • Technical logs — limited operational logs (errors, rate limits, AI usage counts). We aim not to put raw birth charts or chat text into routine logs.

How we use information

  • Calculate chart context and generate AI interpretations you request.
  • Enforce free-response limits and membership access.
  • Send optional daily notes if you enable notifications.
  • Support account linking, recovery, sign-out, and deletion.
  • Secure the service (fraud, abuse, and reliability).

AI processing

Questions and selected chart context are sent to third-party AI providers (currently DeepSeek and/or OpenAI) to generate replies. Those providers process the content under their own terms. Do not submit information you are not comfortable sharing with those processors. Astraly replies are interpretive reflections, not medical, legal, financial, or psychological advice, and not guaranteed predictions.

City search

Birth-city lookup is powered by LocationIQ. Search queries you type for city selection are sent to LocationIQ to return place results. Attribution: Search by LocationIQ.com.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with processors needed to run Astraly (hosting, AI providers, LocationIQ, RevenueCat, Apple, Google) under contractual limits, or when required by law.

Retention and deletion

Guest and account data remain while your account/session is active. You can clear conversation text, sign out, or delete your Astraly account in the app. Deletion removes your profile, chats, chart cache, push tokens, and identity links from our primary database, subject to short-lived backups and legal retention needs.

Children

Astraly is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). Do not use the app if you are under that age.

Your choices

  • Edit or clear birth details and conversations in the app.
  • Decline notifications in system settings.
  • Sign out or delete your account from Profile → Account.
  • Contact us at privacy@astraly.app for privacy requests.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will revise the effective date above and, when changes are material, provide additional notice in the app when practical.
